## [1.0.0] - 2026-08-15

### Added

- Initial public release of AlphaConstruct.
- Reconstruction of transparent images from matching captures against black and white backgrounds.
- Support for linear and sRGB alpha reconstruction.
- Automatic validation of matching source image dimensions.
- Live preview of the reconstructed image on a transparency checkerboard.
- Interactive split preview for comparing reconstruction methods.
- Configurable output folder and filename.
- PNG export with transparency.
- Windows 11 compatible.
- Open-source release under GPL-3.0-or-later.
